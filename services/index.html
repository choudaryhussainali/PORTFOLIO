Questions

Before you ask

The things people actually want to know before they send a brief. If yours is not here, ask it directly — you will get a straight answer, not a sales call.

What does a website actually cost?

The prices are on this page, not behind a call. WordPress starts at PKR 25,000, a hand-built custom site at PKR 35,000, and a Shopify store at PKR 65,000. You get the figure in writing before anything starts, and if the scope grows you get a new quote — never a surprise invoice.

Which one should I pick — WordPress, custom, or Shopify?

WordPress if the budget is tight and you want to change the words yourself. Custom if you want something built only for you that is easy to extend later. Shopify if you are selling products and want the ordering to run without you. If the job is really a repeated task rather than a website, that is Automation. Tell me what you are trying to do and I will point you at the cheapest option that works.

Do I own the code and everything else?

Yes. The source code, the repository and the deployment credentials are handed to you at launch. Nothing sits on my accounts, and you are never locked into me to make a change.

What happens after the site goes live?

Thirty days of support are included with every package — if something breaks in that window it gets fixed at no cost. After that you can take it in-house, or keep me on monthly. There is no automatic renewal you have to remember to cancel.

How do payments work?

Split in two: half to start, half when the site goes live. Bank transfer inside Pakistan, or Wise from overseas. You get an invoice for each payment.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes — most of the sites on this page are. Switch the toggle in the pricing section to see USD. Replies come within 24 hours, and working hours overlap the EU and US-East.

Can you fix the site I already have instead of rebuilding it?

Often, yes. I will look at it first and tell you honestly which is cheaper — repairing what is there or starting again. Sometimes the honest answer is that a rebuild costs you less than patching, and I will say so.

How fast will my site be?

Lighthouse 95+ on mobile is the delivery bar, tested on a mid-range Android over mobile data rather than a fast laptop. This page scores 96 on mobile, so you can check the standard against the site you are reading.
